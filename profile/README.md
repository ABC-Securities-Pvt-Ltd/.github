ABC Securities began operations on 16th Shrawan, 2054 B.S. (25th July 1997) and was among the first 25 firms licensed as stockbrokers by Nepal Stock Exchange Ltd. Nearly three decades later, we remain focused on the same goal: making Nepal's capital market simple and accessible for every investor, wherever they are.

Today, investors can open their trading, DEMAT and MeroShare accounts with us entirely online, from KYC to digital thumbprint to cheque verification, without visiting our office.
