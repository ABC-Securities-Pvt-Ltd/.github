## 👋 About Us

ABC Securities started operations on **16th Shrawan, 2054 B.S. (25th July 1997)** and was among the **first 25 firms** licensed as stockbrokers by Nepal Stock Exchange Ltd. For nearly three decades we've worked to make investing in Nepal's capital market simpler, fairer and more accessible, and today we're bringing that experience fully online.

## 💼 What We Offer

| | Service | Description |
|:-:|---|---|
| 📈 | **Trading Account** | Buy and sell shares, bonds and more through a licensed broker (NEPSE No. 17). |
| 🏦 | **DEMAT Account** | Hold your securities electronically, like a bank account for stocks, bonds and mutual funds. |
| 🔐 | **MeroShare** | Securely manage your securities, apply for IPOs and track your holdings. |
